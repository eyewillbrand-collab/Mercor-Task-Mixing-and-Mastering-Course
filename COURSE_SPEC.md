# Course scope

Seven sequential modules: organization, gain staging, mixing, mastering, publishing, marketing/SEO, release readiness.

The numeric practice checkpoints are deterministic educational examples, not measurements of actual audio or proof of professional competency. The local practice summary can be edited by anyone with access to the browser and is not a verified credential. Future work: real audio analysis with test fixtures, interactive signal flow, validated rubrics, human listening evaluation, accessible PDF reporting and verifiable certificates. Never treat a fixed LUFS value as a universal mastering target.

No private student information or external service credentials are needed for this prototype.
